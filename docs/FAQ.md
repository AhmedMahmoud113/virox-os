# Questions people ask

**The usage the router shows is a little different from my internet company's app. Why?**
The router counts everything that passed through the line; the company counts in its own way and its
app often updates late. If the difference is big: open **Usage**, type the remaining amount the
company's app shows, and save. The package goes on from the company's figure until the renewal. With
two lines, do it for each line in **Settings → Lines and packages**.

**What are "The router itself" and "Unknown" in the usage?**
"The router itself" is the router's own use (setting its clock, looking up site names, updates).
"Unknown" is use that could not be given to a device; it is usually very small.

**My child changes the phone's device number (MAC) to get around the rules.**
Open **Control → New devices** and choose **Ask me first**. A new device then gets no internet until
you allow it. If it was the same phone, open it on the Devices page and choose **This is the same device as** under **More**. See
also [PHONE-MAC.md](PHONE-MAC.md).

**Kids mode is on but some sites still open.**
Check that kids mode is on for that exact device (not another device of the same child), and that
**Ask me first** is on. Kids mode closes the usual ways around the filter (other or encrypted DNS);
for VPN apps, also switch on **Ways around the filter (VPN, proxy, encrypted DNS services)** in **Control → House filter**.

**How can a family member see only their own usage?**
**Settings → Family logins**: add a login with a name and password and choose their person. They see
only their devices and usage, can ask for more time, and cannot change anything else.

**There is a new version. How do I install it?**
A notice appears on the home page. Open **Settings → Update** and click **Install now**. Your settings
stay; the internet stops for about 5 minutes. Do not switch the router off during that time. With
access points, **Update all** updates every unit one after the other.

**I want to save my settings before a big change.**
**Settings → Help and backup → Save my settings**. The file contains the Wi-Fi passwords: keep it
private. With a USB stick in the router, a copy is also saved every night by itself.

**Someone helping me asks for a report.**
**Settings → Help and backup → Download the help report**. The report has no passwords, no device
names and no addresses.
